Capabilities are visible.
Effects are not.

The policy problem is the rift between them.

AI capability

What systems can produce

Model evaluations, access logs, and red-teaming make the upstream side comparatively observable.

Deployment

Capability is not deployment

Risk depends on routes, operators, data, tools, permissions, distribution, repetition, and scale. These are gates, not footnotes.

Behavioural effect

The causal chain crosses a rift

Authentic exposure, attention, beliefs, trust, intentions, choices, and dependency are separate links. Each can attenuate, adapt, or fail.

Aggregate consequence

The farthest claim is the weakest

Electoral outcomes are distant, diffuse, confounded, and hard to attribute to any single intervention.

Evidence interface

Measure the node that changed

Preserve route identity, delivery denominators, interruption history, evidence custody, missingness, and the claim ceiling needed for the next bridge.

Core governance rule

Act upstream.
Measure downstream.

Do not promote capability into effect. Do not wait for changed votes before acting on an evidenced mechanism.
