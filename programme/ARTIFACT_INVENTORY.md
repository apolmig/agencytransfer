# Canonical artifact inventory

**Inventory date:** 28 August 2026  
**Purpose:** establish one programme record before redesigning the public site.

The immediate problem is not lack of material. It is version and role confusion. The benchmark currently presents itself as the whole project; several documents compete for “final” status; source and public dataset versions differ; visual variants are not clearly archived; and video masters do not yet have durable canonical hosting.

The decisions below are the publication baseline.

## Part III — Election evidence

**Canonical public dataset:** `apol/ai-election-manipulation-cases`  
**Source record:** source-integrated v0.3.1  
**Public release:** v0.4.4  
**Research cutoff:** 12 August 2026

Counts must always appear in this order:

> 1,087 relational rows → 64 catalogue entries → 10 core records → 8 incident-eligible records → 6 documented-manipulation records

Relational rows and catalogue entries are not cases. The public release contains six documented-manipulation records.

The primary visual should be a count funnel plus an evidence matrix by case: occurrence, mechanism, attribution, distribution, authentic exposure, human effect, institutional response, and electoral effect. A decorative world map would overstate geographic coverage and invite prevalence claims the corpus cannot support.

## Explainers and media

One repository-backed video artifact was located: the Brazil 2026 harmful-manipulation explainer. The repository points to temporary MP4 hosting. That is not acceptable as the programme’s canonical public record.

Before publication it needs:

1. a durable public host or first-party asset URL;
2. a preserved private master;
3. final title, date, language, and duration;
4. a poster frame;
5. captions and a transcript;
6. an explicit synthetic-scenario label;
7. a dual-use review of the final cut; and
8. a claim boundary stating that it is not evidence of a real operation, exposure, behaviour change, or electoral effect.

Additional explainers and presentation artifacts have been reported but were not found with stable, unambiguous source locations during this inventory. They remain an explicit backlog rather than being guessed into the public programme.

## Canonical-link rules

- The public repository and programme site are canonical.
- The public Agency Transfer Lab deployment may be linked; its private source repository may not.
- Private controlled-evidence repositories may not be linked.
- Legacy copies under personal-site repositories are mirrors, not new outputs.
- Temporary file hosts are never canonical.
- A Drive filename, upload date, or “final” suffix does not establish canonical status by itself.
- Each public output receives one canonical URL; variants move to an archive.
