# Classic illustrations in the research publication

The publication reuses the author's existing artwork from `Cambridge-ERA / Images`. These are conceptual illustrations, not empirical charts, evidence of a completed operation, or validated policy packages.

## Page assignment

- **Homepage:** the approved Capability–Deployment–Effect Gap hero remains unchanged. It is the programme-level visual and is not repeated elsewhere as decoration.
- **Part I and draft paper overview:** **Anatomy of an AI Manipulation Operation** explains the possible operating chain and where agency and safeguards enter. It replaces the flat mechanism SVG.
- **Part II:** the Evaluation Registry remains an independent artifact and is not modified by this visual pass.
- **Part III:** no large conceptual illustration. The page stays focused on source-linked field evidence and claim boundaries.
- **Part IV:** **Where Interventions Act—and Where Their Evidence Stops** is the primary evidence map. It distinguishes policy coverage from checked component-effect evidence.
- **Artifacts:** **Democratic Circuit Breakers** serves as an engaging navigation map from detection and review to response, evidence and remedy. Its desired outcomes are explicitly labelled as intentions, not findings.
- **Explainers:** the animation remains the dominant visual. The earlier repeated mechanism SVG is removed; Part I carries the full anatomy illustration.

The originals retain their wording and composition. Responsive WebP variants are the only public image derivatives. Source names, dimensions, byte lengths and SHA-256 values are recorded in `classic-artwork.json`.

All essential interpretation and limitations remain in HTML. Small labels inside the artwork are therefore not the only explanation available to mobile or assistive-technology users. Full-size links use ordinary browser navigation and no gallery dependency.
