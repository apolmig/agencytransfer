# Classic illustrations in the research publication

The publication reuses the author's existing PNG artwork from Cambridge-ERA / Images. These are conceptual illustrations, not data charts, evidence of a completed operation, or validated policy packages.

- **Anatomy of an AI Manipulation Operation** replaces the flat mechanism SVG in Part I and the repeated CDE image in the paper overview.
- **Democratic Circuit Breakers** appears in Part IV and after the artifact directory. Its reading guide separates detection, response and remedy. It explicitly labels the desired downstream outcomes as intentions, not findings.
- The earlier policy-atlas composite was considered but not selected: its old checked-effect/backlog numbers should not be presented as current findings.

The original images retain their wording, composition and transparency. Responsive WebP variants are the only new image files. Source names, dimensions, byte lengths and SHA-256 values are in `classic-artwork.json`. No Drive folder permissions were changed.

The homepage hero, the independent Registry (including its own charts and imagery), empirical data, bibliography and manuscript files are not modified. The paper remains an unnumbered unpublished draft with its full text coming soon.

Full-size links use ordinary browser navigation, without a gallery dependency. All essential reading guidance is HTML, so small image labels are not the sole explanation for mobile or assistive-technology users.
