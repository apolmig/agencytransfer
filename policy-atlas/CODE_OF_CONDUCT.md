# Code of conduct

## Standard

Participants must engage with evidence and with one another in good faith.
Expected conduct includes:

- criticizing claims, methods, and evidence without attacking people;
- stating uncertainty, limitations, and relevant conflicts;
- welcoming correction and documenting disagreement accurately;
- respecting privacy, attribution, consent, and source licences;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing,
  retaliation, and sustained disruption; and
- handling politically sensitive material without partisan targeting or
  dehumanizing language.

Good-faith methodological disagreement is not misconduct. Fabricating sources,
misrepresenting review, concealing a material conflict, or using project access
to facilitate manipulation may lead to contribution rejection or loss of
access in addition to correction of the research record.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, project meetings,
release work, and public interactions where someone represents the Policy
Atlas.

Until those fields are completed, do not place sensitive allegations or
personal information in a public issue. A non-sensitive moderation request may
be raised with the lead maintainer through the repository. This interim state
is a governance limitation of the beta, not a promise of confidential case
handling.

When a report can be handled safely, the responsible human should:

1. acknowledge it without prejudging the outcome;
2. identify conflicts and recuse where necessary;
3. preserve relevant evidence with minimal access;
4. give affected parties a fair opportunity to respond where safe;
5. choose a proportionate response; and
6. record the decision while protecting personal information.

Possible responses include clarification, content moderation, a warning,
contribution rejection, temporary restriction, removal of access, correction
of the research record, or referral to the relevant platform or authority.
Retaliation against a reporter or witness is prohibited.
