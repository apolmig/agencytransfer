  proposed stable-core candidates for intensive verification and explicitly
  limits the effect-direction field to a process declaration;
- retains the exact 88-row complement as a candidate registry and marks every
  implementation `stable_core_ready=false` and `ranking_ready=false`;
- publishes 13 machine-readable stable-release gates and their current
  blockers;

  repo-only placeholder templates; no scores or rank results are published;
- adds governance, contribution, correction, maintainership, conduct, and
  explicit data/code licensing files;

  performs no network action, and cannot mint a DOI;

  type; legal-source checks no longer count as mechanism verification;

  them as provenance in normalized tables, and sets the authoritative public
  posture to `not_assessed`;
- does not upgrade any legal, mechanism, or effect claim.
