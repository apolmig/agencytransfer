## Scope

118 implementations are annotated against 69 selected source records. Eight records received new substantive/main-text checks; other source depths are individually recorded and six earlier adjudications are retained. Three new candidates remain outside the 118-row denominator. This is a single-pass, purposive assessment, not independent double screening, an exhaustive current-law review, or reproduction of all source data.

H (10), T (12), O (18), A (27) and N (51) describe human, technical, implementation/quasi-experimental, adjacent, and normative/design/measurement evidence respectively. They are not ordered grades or counts of policies that work. Multiple source matches are not independent experiments.

The prior A–F groups are retained as historical metadata. The recommendations and source-match columns in this review supersede that presentation for the website. The production Atlas beta.3 data, six checked empirical-effect relations, and existing evidence grades are unchanged. The latest working manuscript is v1.3, which integrates this review. Parts I–III retain their 28 August freeze; P4 is dated 1 September. The website links the exact v1.3 PDF.

The three source files are copied without substantive alteration. [review.json](review.json) records their SHA-256 digests, original names, source IDs, coverage, and source-review depths. The concise website text is an editorial synthesis, not a new independent review.
