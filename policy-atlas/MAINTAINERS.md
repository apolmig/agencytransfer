## Becoming or leaving a maintainer

The lead maintainer appoints beta maintainers based on sustained, reviewable
contributions and relevant competence. Appointment must be recorded by pull
request with role, scope, conflicts, and effective date.

A maintainer may resign at any time. Access may be suspended for security,
conduct, persistent undisclosed conflicts, or repeated disregard of the
evidence rules. The reason and effective date should be recorded without
publishing sensitive personal information. Removing repository access does not
erase authorship or prior review history.
